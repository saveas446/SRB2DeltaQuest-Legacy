Demo 2 Ultimate is a mod of SRB2 Demo 2 that improves the assets, code, and more,
finishes the intended 32-map campaign for SRB2, and adds five more additional secret levels.

It is currently in development and has been since February/March 2024.

The project is about 60% done, but progress is being made quickly and hopefully it will be done within a couple months.
