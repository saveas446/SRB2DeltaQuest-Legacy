Various MIDIs that never got used in any version of the game with one exception.
These were all either released by a developer or composer who worked on SRB2. The
aforementioned exception is System Shock - Intro (Techno Hill Zone; DOS).mid.
This song in particular was used in, as the filename suggests SRB2 DOS, which was
created by Stealth. Why didn't we lump this with the SRB2 TGF MIDIs? Simple, the
version Stealth used was a recorded MIDI. Massive props to Vertz1515 for the
find!
